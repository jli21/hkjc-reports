# Research

Three documents, and the differences between them matter.

* **[findings.md](findings.md)** -- what was tried and what it showed, in the order it happened.
  A log, kept as evidence. Sections whose numbers have been superseded carry a note saying so
  rather than being rewritten.
* **[backlog.md](backlog.md)** -- what is still open, and why each item is open rather than
  done.
* **[memory.md](memory.md)** -- research questions, hypotheses and external data-source leads
  that are not open work yet: conditional IIA and race interactions, sparse local history and
  cold starts, external identity sources and their resolution rules, a cold-start model ladder,
  reliability and missingness, and the leakage controls a vendor proof of concept would need.
  Nothing in it is a promoted result. Moved here from `RESEARCH_MEMORY.md` at the repository
  root on 2026-08-31.

The current published numbers are not here. They are in the four model reports under
`results/reports/`, generated from declared artifacts with their inputs locked by digest. Where
a document in this directory disagrees with a report, the report is right.

For the design decisions behind the pipeline itself, see [../plans/index.md](../plans/index.md).
