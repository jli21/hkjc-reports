8. Limitations, multiple-search accounting, prospective test

  • Multiplicity. 28 blocks × 2 productions were screened; the simultaneous max-t critical value over wave 1 is 2.81, over wave 2 2.85, over wave 3 2.45, over wave 4 2.35. No single-block interval that merely excludes zero would survive that; none did.
  • The screen is a linear head. A one-parameter multiplier cannot express an interaction the booster might find; that is why odds-independent rejections were carried to canonical as validation arms rather than trusted (section 5).
  • P0 is the anchored link, not native probit training. The plan's probit_verification module is absent from this checkout; the link is the verified object (report/probit-anchored.md). A native-probit P0 would be a different instrument.
  • History depth. Study I targets begin in 2013 and forward residuals in 2016, so F1/F2 histories are short at the left edge of the canonical years (defined shares are in the tables). Reference-engine states carry full history.
  • Every year has been development data for some campaign in this repository; the canonical years are not untouched holdouts. Nothing here is promotion-eligible; that requires a separately locked prospective shadow test.
  • Prospective test proposal. Freeze any research-survivor's exact formula and constants now (they are in the manifests), materialise it live from the same sources for the 2026-27 season, and score paired R − C under both productions on races not yet run. Only that converts a retrospective survivor into production information.

Why this cycle stops here, and what a fourth wave would need

The plan's stop rule is an empty backlog of mechanistically distinct, data-supported ideas. After three waves: (i) every level of past performance available in this repository is priced (wave 1); (ii) the odds-independent departures are null except the return-state family (waves 1-2); (iii) that family's stated mechanism was tested and refuted, and it does not sharpen (wave 3). Ideas that remain are not data-supported here: a return × recent-trial interaction is derivable from production's own bt_days_since and layoff_log (redundancy, not signal, per plan §7); trackwork and veterinary records are cohort-selected recent-era tables the build deliberately excludes; pedigree/origin and jockey booking are closed by Studies I and V. The next investment is the prospective test above or new data capture, not a 26th block.
